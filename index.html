Our Legacy

Nurturing Spiritual Growth in Dublin

Originally established as the Dawah Community Centre, the Shuhada Foundation has grown into a vital hub for the local Muslim community in Dublin 15.

Operating under the Irish Islamic Dawah and Education Trust (CHY 18219), we focus on providing a harmonious environment for worship, youth education at the Shahida Zainab School, and community welfare.
