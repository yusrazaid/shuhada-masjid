Our Legacy

Nurturing Spiritual Growth in Dublin Since 2008

Originally established as the Dawah Community Centre, the Shuhada Foundation has grown into a vital hub for the local Muslim community in Dublin 15.

Operating under the Irish Islamic Dawah and Education Trust (CHY 18219), we focus on providing a harmonious environment for worship, youth education, and community welfare, building a foundation for future generations.

Community Services

Dedicated to facilitating comprehensive support for the spiritual, educational, and social needs of our diverse community.

Daily Prayers

Sports Field

Spacious outdoor facilities regularly utilized for community football, cricket, and engaging youth activities.

On-site Parking

Extensive and secure parking facilities boasting a 200-car capacity to accommodate large gatherings safely.
